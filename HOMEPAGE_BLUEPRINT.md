## MOBILE LAYOUT NOTES

On mobile (< 768px):
- Nav collapses to **hamburger** (☰) with slide-out drawer
- Category tiles stack to **2 per row** (instead of 7)
- Trending marquee, carousels, occasion grid → **horizontal swipe**
- Editorial split stacks **vertically** (Men on top, Women below)
- Build Your Own steps stay horizontal but compress
- Footer columns collapse to **accordions**
- WhatsApp button stays floating bottom-right

## DESIGN SYSTEM (quick reference)

**Color palette:** charcoal black `#0E0C0A` + cream `#FAF8F5` + cognac `#8B5A2B` + gold `#D4A24A` + sale red `#C0392B`

**Typography:** Playfair Display (serif headlines) + Inter (sans body)

**Vertical spacing:** 100-120px desktop / 64-72px mobile per section

**Performance & A11y:** lazy-load below-the-fold images, hero video has poster fallback, all CTAs ≥ 44px tap target, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, alt text on every image
