## DESIGN SYSTEM

### Color Palette (luxury-meets-moto)
- `#0E0C0A` — bg dark (Prada/AllSaints inky black)
- `#FAF8F5` — bg light (cream)
- `#F1ECE4` — bg alt (parchment)
- `#1A1A1A` — primary ink
- `#8B5A2B` — accent (cognac leather)
- `#C69A6B` — accent soft
- `#D4A24A` — gold (stars/awards)
- `#C0392B` — sale red (urgency)
- `#2E7D32` — verified green
- `#25D366` — WhatsApp green

### Typography
- **Headlines:** "Playfair Display" or "Canela" (serif, luxury)
- **Body:** "Inter" or "Söhne" (sans-serif, modern)
- **Eyebrows:** uppercase, letter-spaced, 12px

### Section spacing
- Desktop: 100-120px vertical padding per section
- Mobile: 64-72px

### Performance & A11y
- Lazy-load all below-the-fold imagery
- Hero video has poster fallback
- All CTAs ≥ 44px tap target
- `prefers-reduced-motion` respected
- Alt text on every image
