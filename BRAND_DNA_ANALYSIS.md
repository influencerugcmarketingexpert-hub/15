## MERGED BRAND PERSONALITY (positioning of our hybrid site)

> **"Heritage-rooted, technically-engineered, custom-built leather — for life, road, and runway."**

Three pillars:
1. **Luxury heritage** (Prada × Overland × AllSaints)
2. **Technical & safety** (Alpinestars × RST)
3. **Personalization & value** (Inland × ThursdayBoots × JacketMaker × Artisan × Trendy × Buffalo)

This dictates the homepage flow: cinematic hero → category split → custom builder → technical proof → deals → community.
